# Design Tokens — My Arborist!

Forked from Template Zero. Full re-run of the token brainstorm — the sixth and final distinct direction in this batch.

## 1. Identity

Salvador Ortiz, My Arborist!, Denton TX — 5.0★ across 122 reviews (by far the largest review base in this batch), 10 years' experience, and — verified directly on his existing site, myarborist.net — **three real ISA certifications**: ISA Certified Arborist (TX-3947AUM), ISA Certified Arborist Utility Specialist, and ISA Certified Urban Forest Professional. His current site (myarborist.net) is functional but dated — a standard early-2020s small-business template with circular-photo testimonials and no real visual identity. This demo needs to make that contrast obvious rather than iterate on it.

## 2. Palette concept

Rejected generic "arborist green" in favor of a palette that reads as a credential document — a technical, authoritative register distinct from the more casual/natural greens used elsewhere in this batch (Master Bin, Mendoza, Clark's). Pine Ink is deliberately cooler and darker than those, and Certified Amber stands in for the color of an official seal or stamp, not a trend accent.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Pine Ink** | `#1B2E27` | Base dark surface — cooler/darker than this batch's other greens, on purpose |
| **Paper** | `#F7F5EF` | Base light surface — a technical-document white, not a warm craft-brand cream |
| **Ink** | `#16241F` | Body text on light surfaces |
| **Certified Amber** | `#C77D2E` | Primary accent — the color of an official seal/stamp |
| **Field Green** | `#4F7A5C` | Secondary accent — data labels, stats |
| **Slate** | `#8A928C` | Muted support — borders, unstamped credential outlines |

## 3. Type pairing

- **Display:** Space Grotesk (600/700) — modern, technical, authoritative without being cold.
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans — clean, professional, documentation-grade.
- **Third tier, used sparingly:** IBM Plex Mono — reserved for credential numbers, data labels, and stats (the eyebrow tags, spec-sheet labels, service tags), reinforcing the "technical record" feel in the specific places precision matters.

**Why a three-tier type system:** none of the other five businesses needed a technical/mono layer — their identities were about craft, simplicity, transformation, or personality, not documentation. My Arborist!'s biggest asset is verifiable, numbered credentials, so treating that data typographically differently from prose headlines is the whole point.

## 4. Signature element: The Credential Stack

**None** of this batch's other signature devices fit: not a fill-bar (too generic), not a checklist (recurring-service specific), not a brushline (too minimal for this much real credential data), not a canopy background (too naturalistic), not an oversized-type gimmick (undermines the authoritative tone). Instead: three small circular seals fixed on the left edge — literally representing Salvador's three real certifications — that stamp solid one at a time as you scroll down the page. It's the only device in the batch built directly from a specific, countable, real fact about the business (three certifications, not a metaphor).

## 5. Structural choices

- **Layout style assigned:** a rigorous "spec sheet" / field-report structure — a definition-list-style credentials panel and a two-column technical services list, both distinct from every card grid, checklist, or masonry layout used elsewhere in this batch.
- **Lead section:** Credentials, directly under the hero — per the brief, this is explicitly the business's biggest differentiator from unlicensed competitors, so it leads rather than living in an About section.
- **Reviews:** 122 reviews is the largest base in the batch, so unlike Master Bin's minimal honesty-first approach, this section states the number prominently — but still uses a single consensus paragraph rather than inventing named quotes, since exact review text with attribution wasn't independently verifiable the way it was for three other businesses in this batch (Yelp and HomeAdvisor both blocked scraping with a 403). The consensus language ("courteous, knowledgeable... tremendous communication") is a fair paraphrase of what search results characterized reviews as saying, explicitly not presented as a direct quote.
- **About:** moderate length — credentials are the headline draw here (unlike Clark's, where personality was), but Salvador's decade of tenure still earns a real paragraph.
- **Contrast with the existing site:** deliberately avoids myarborist.net's circular-photo-testimonial layout and generic small-business template feel, per the brief's explicit instruction.

## 6. Images

Real stock (Unsplash) — a tree-survey/measuring shot (directly matches the "Tree Surveys & Consultations" service and the credential-forward identity) and a safety-rigged climbing shot for the About section. No AI-generated imagery.

## 7. Flag for Owen

None. Research corroborated the brief's facts (credentials, experience, rating) directly from Salvador's own existing site — no independent concerns surfaced.
