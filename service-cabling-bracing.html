Services / Support

Cabling & Bracing

For a tree that's structurally compromised but still worth saving — a split trunk, a heavy leaning limb, co-dominant stems — cabling and bracing hardware redistributes the load so the tree can be kept rather than removed. It's the alternative to removal when removal isn't actually necessary.
